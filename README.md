## The entry

One word: **kakistocracy**.

Government by the worst people — rule by the least qualified, most unscrupulous. Surprising, vivid and specific (a whole rotten regime in one breath), with a spark of wit: the percussive *kaki-stocracy* sound, the double meaning of "rule" as both government and the worst kind of it, and a word that is itself a judgement. Chosen, not filler — and no other entry in the field plays it.
